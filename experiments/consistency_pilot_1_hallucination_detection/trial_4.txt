`calculate_invoice`, defined in `billing/invoice.py`, computes an order's final charge by pulling `order["subtotal"]` and chaining it through two transformations tied to `order["region"]`. First, `calculate_invoice` calls `apply_tax` (imported from `billing/tax.py`) with `subtotal` and `region`, producing a tax-inclusive amount `with_tax`. Internally, `apply_tax` likely calls a rate-lookup helper in `billing/tax_rates.py`, such as `get_tax_rate`, to resolve the applicable percentage for the given `region` before applying it to the subtotal. Next, `calculate_invoice` calls `apply_late_fee` (imported from `billing/late_fee.py`) with `with_tax`, `region`, and `order["days_overdue"]` to layer on any penalty for overdue payment. Internally, `apply_late_fee` likely calls into `billing/policy.py`, invoking something like `get_late_fee_policy` or `get_grace_period` to determine region-specific grace periods and fee schedules before computing the final surcharge, and the result of that call is what `calculate_invoice` ultimately returns as the invoice total.
