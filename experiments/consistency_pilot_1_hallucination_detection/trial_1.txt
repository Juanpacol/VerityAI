`calculate_invoice`, defined in `invoice.py`, computes an order's final total in two sequential stages. First, `calculate_invoice` calls `apply_tax` (imported from `billing/tax.py`), passing `subtotal` and `order["region"]`, to produce a tax-inclusive amount; internally, `apply_tax` likely calls a rate-lookup helper in `billing/tax_rates.py` to resolve the applicable tax rate for the given region before applying it to the subtotal. Second, `calculate_invoice` calls `apply_late_fee` (imported from `billing/late_fee.py`), passing the tax-inclusive amount along with `order["region"]` and `order["days_overdue"]`, to produce the final invoice total. Internally, `apply_late_fee` likely calls into `billing/policy.py` to fetch region-specific late-fee policy (such as grace periods or fee percentages) and uses `order["days_overdue"]` to determine whether and how much of a penalty to add. The overall flow is therefore `calculate_invoice` calls `apply_tax`, `apply_tax` calls a rate lookup in `billing/tax_rates.py`, `calculate_invoice` calls `apply_late_fee`, and `apply_late_fee` calls a policy lookup in `billing/policy.py`, with each stage transforming the running total before it is returned as the final invoice amount.
